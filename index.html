Methodology

Draft. These figures are estimates and will change after the next review.

What you are looking at

Each bubble is a US news or commentary channel. Teal bubbles are big media companies. Peach bubbles are independent channels run by creators or small teams. The bigger the bubble, the more people it reaches each month.

How we worked out the 2026 sizes

  • Media companies: we took each one's 2025 size and moved it up or down by how much its TV viewers and website visitors changed in a year.
  • Independent channels from our 2025 research: we took each one's 2025 size and moved it up or down by how much its YouTube views changed in a year.
  • Newer independent channels: we took their monthly YouTube views and counted about half as people, because the same viewers often watch several videos.
  • Channels we could not measure keep their 2025 size for now.

How the growth is drawn

Each bubble grows in a straight line from the year it started to its 2026 size. Outlets older than 2000 start at their 2000 size instead. Real growth went up and down more than this.

Where the numbers come from

TV ratings (Nielsen), website traffic (Comscore and Similarweb), YouTube views (vidIQ) and the ZuluLens 2025 research.

The "Sheet formula" switch

This shows the 2025 research channels sized the same way as the newer ones, from their YouTube views alone. It is there for comparison.

Keep in mind

  • These are estimates for comparing channels, not exact counts.
  • Newer channels count YouTube only, so podcast listeners are missing.
  • Most viewers are in the US, but some are abroad.
  • A few small channels still use placeholder figures.
  • Political leans are simplified labels.
